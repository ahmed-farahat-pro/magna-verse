How the index is calculated

The Herfindahl–Hirschman index is the sum of squared vendor shares, where each share is that vendor's committed value over total committed value:

H= n∑i = 1 si2 , si= vi V KaTeX · display

With n = 37 vendors the raw index comes out at H = 0.1912. Because a raw H is floored by the vendor count, the committee should see the normalised form, which strips out the 1n floor and rescales to a 0–1 range:

H*= H − 1n 1 − 1n = 0.1912 − 0.0270 1 − 0.0270 =0.1687 KaTeX · display
0.1687 sits just under your 0.18 escalation line — but Al-Rashid alone carries 34 sole-source lines. Under §4.2 of the Procurement Concentration Policy, sole-source density above 30 lines triggers review regardless of the index.2 So this does go to the committee, on the sole-source clause rather than on HHI.

What I would put on the slide

  • Split the remaining Package 4 award 60/40 rather than renewing single-source — this alone moves H* to roughly 0.141.
  • Qualify two alternate steel fabricators; both appear in the 2025 pre-qualification register with current ISO 3834 certification.3
  • Accept a three-week tender window in the P6 baseline. Package 4 currently holds 11 days of total float, so this is not free — it eats the float and pushes the steel-complete milestone to 04 Nov 2026.4

Fourteen more days consumes the 11 days of float and puts seven activities on the critical path. Three of them have downstream commissioning dependencies that cannot be resequenced:4

  • A-4120 Erect grid lines 7–11 — becomes critical, −3 days float, drags HV switchgear energisation.

  • A-4210 Fire-proofing to columns — −7 days, and it is the predecessor to the authority inspection booked for 18 Nov.

This is the query I ran, if you want it in the weekly pack:

SQLp6_slip_scenario.sql 
-- Package 4 float exposure after a 14-day fabrication slip
SELECT  a.activity_id, a.name,
        a.total_float_days                    AS float_now,
        a.total_float_days - 14              AS float_after,
        a.early_finish, a.late_finish
FROM    p6.activity a
JOIN    p6.wbs w ON w.wbs_id = a.wbs_id
WHERE   w.package = 'PKG-04-STEEL'
  AND   a.total_float_days <= 25
  AND   a.status <> 'Completed'
ORDER BY float_after ASC;
