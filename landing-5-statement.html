02 / The argument

Sovereignty is not a compliance checkbox. It is who holds the capability.

A residency clause is a promise about someone else's datacentre. A rack in Riyadh is a fact about yours. The question a Saudi enterprise should be asking is not where is our data stored — it is whose machines does our thinking run on.

  • Runs on

    Magna hardware, in-country. MagnaREACTOR AI POD — NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 liquid-cooled racks. A typical workspace tenancy is 8×H200 in Riyadh DC-1. On-prem, sovereign cloud, or edge; data residency guaranteed in-country.

  • Leaves

    Nothing. No prompt, no document, no embedding, no retrieval, no log. A private-tier answer makes no outbound call — there is no public API in the path to trust.

  • Stays

    Every token, and the record of it. Inference, context, and the full audit trail remain inside your perimeter, under policy you set. ISO 27001 aligned, red-team audited, 24/7 managed operations across five global regions.

03 / What it actually is

Not a chatbot with a compliance page. A workspace, with the whole company plugged into it.

  1. 01

    The workspace

    Conversational, familiar on the first day, no training programme required. Behind it: Kimi K2 Instruct (1T MoE), DeepSeek V3.1 (671B MoE), Qwen 3 235B and NVIDIA Nemotron 120B — all run privately on Magna GPUs.

  2. 02

    80+ agents

    Pre-built agents across 34 verticals — construction, energy, manufacturing, government — plus workflow automation, so the work leaves the chat window and actually happens.

  3. 03

    Your systems

    Enterprise connectors to SAP S/4HANA, Primavera P6, Jira, Confluence and mail, joined over MCP. The workspace reads your systems of record in place — nothing is copied out to a vendor's index.

  4. 04

    A digital twin

    A context graph of the operation itself — projects, assets, contracts, people — so an answer is grounded in your company rather than in the open internet.

  5. 05

    Governance that holds

    Policy-enforced tiers with full audit trails. The CISO sets what a tier may reach; the system enforces it, and writes down that it did. 1,000+ validated use cases to date.
