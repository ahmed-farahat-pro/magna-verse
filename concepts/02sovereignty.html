What changed & why

Sovereignty is the reason to buy Magna instead of ChatGPT. Right now it is a dropdown.

1

Tiers explain themselves

Today: four options, two of them dead “Coming soon” rows, described in one abstract line each.
Now: only real choices, each stating where it runs, what leaves, which models and what you give up.

2

The guardrail is the feature

Nothing today stops a user moving a thread full of SAP data to Public. A pre-flight check that names the exact files at risk is more valuable than any settings page.

3

Per-answer provenance

Today: a global “Private” pill in the header, repeated twice, that tells you nothing about a given answer.
Now: every answer carries where it ran and what it touched.

4

A receipt you can hand to audit

Node, egress bytes, tools, PII masking, retention, signed hash. This is what a CISO signs off on — and it costs nothing to show, because the platform already logs it in Langfuse.

5

Show the boundary

One small diagram does more for trust than the word “Private”. Public cloud is drawn and visibly not contacted.
