What changed & why

The current home screen is a poster. This one is a place to start work.

1

Status, not slogan

Today: a giant “MagnaVERSE — your enterprise operational brain” over a city photo.
Now: where you are, what’s connected, what ran while you slept. The tagline belongs on the marketing site.

2

Chips draft, they don’t fire

Today: clicking Analyze silently sends a 30-word question on your behalf. That is the single most surprising thing in the app.
Now: it fills an editable brief with slots.

3

Resume beats restart

Threads, paused tasks and overnight scheduler results share one row. Today an overnight “Heartbeat” run is invisible unless you go hunting for it.

4

Starters from real connections

Generic prompt suggestions teach nothing. Suggestions generated from your SAP, KB and Jira teach people what an on-prem brain is actually for — and each unconnected slot becomes a sales hook.

5

Promote the command palette

It already exists behind an unlabelled magnifier and is the best thing in the product. Give it a visible ⌘K entry point and let it search documents and actions, not just page names.

6

Make MCPs discoverable with /

Today: MCP servers are reachable only through Tools → {} MCP, with no explanation of what any of them do or when they fire.
Now: typing / in the composer opens one filterable menu of skills and MCP servers — sap-po-lookup, jira-search, p6-schedule — each with a plain-language description. Arrow keys navigate, ↵ inserts, Esc closes and returns focus to the composer.
