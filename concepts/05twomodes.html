What changed & why

Half of MagnaVERSE’s navigation is currently a promise, and the developer tooling is in the user’s face.

1

Count the dead ends

Clicking through every flyout: 26 destinations, 13 disabled. A new user’s first impression is a product that mostly doesn’t work yet — even though the parts that do work are strong.

2

“Upcoming” is highlighted by default

The one nav section boxed and accented on load contains five items, all disabled. Roadmap belongs in Help or a changelog, never in primary navigation.

3

Three modes, not one menu

A procurement lead and a platform engineer are being shown the same 26 items. Work / Build / Govern lets each see a product sized for them.

4

Six items beats twenty-six

Also fixes concrete confusions found in testing: Workspace and Apps share an identical cube icon; the page titled Tasks is subtitled “custom roles for delegation” and its button says + New agent; the account menu hides behind a gear.

5

Move the dev tooling out

Langfuse trace IDs, FINAL RETURN, a broken elon-musk-perspective-en (failed to parse SKILL…) entry, raw memory keys and HSL sliders are all shipping to end users today. They belong behind Admin.

6

Settings, rebuilt plainly

Today: Appearance ends in raw hsl(210, 67%, 47%) sliders, film grain, vignette, wallpaper zoom and seven background animations.
Now: the Claude/ChatGPT idiom — a modal, categories on the left, plain rows on the right. It does not need to look fascinating; it needs to be understood. Anything a user cannot evaluate moves to Admin or goes away.
