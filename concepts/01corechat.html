What changed & why

Five fixes to the highest-frequency screen in the product.

1

History becomes furniture

Today: chats hide behind Chat → History → expand, in a 150px box with titles cut to 18 characters.
Now: a permanent rail, full titles, date groups, and a dot per thread showing which sovereignty tier it ran in.

2

Reasoning collapses to one line

Today: a Langfuse-style step table (FINAL RETURN · model · 7.8s) sits expanded above every answer and pushes it below the fold.
Now: one chip, expandable.

3

Citations, split web vs internal

Today: answers name studies in prose with no links.
Now: numbered inline cites and a source list that visibly separates public web from your own KB — the whole point of a sovereign deployment.

4

Message actions people expect

Today: three unlabelled icons, no tooltips, and no copy button.
Now: Copy, Retry, Save, Export, Share, feedback — labelled.

5

One readable surface

Today: body text sits on a Riyadh skyline photo at 50% overlay; the robot companion overlaps the send button.
Now: flat surface, labelled composer chips, visible shortcut hints.

6

No global header

Today: a full-width bar carries logo, search, sovereignty, bank, bell, info and gear.
Now: it is gone. Search moves into the sidebar, sovereignty and model become per-thread controls — the ChatGPT/Claude pattern. Model names are written in full: Kimi K2 Instruct · 1T MoE, never “Latest Kimi”.
