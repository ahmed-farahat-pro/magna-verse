How these marks were made

Escape closes this.

Works
The whole happy path is built and clickable in at least one of the six prototypes.
Partial
Most of the path runs, but a named step does not — the answer arrives and the artefact does not, or the tool exists and the worked example does not.
Gap
The flow cannot complete at all. Nothing in the prototypes does this.

Evidence. Each of the six prototypes was opened and driven — signed in, workspace picked, Connections, Agents, Tasks, Knowledge and Mail walked. Where a judgement turns on a detail (does a chart render? is the artifact editable? does the mail stepper complete?) it was checked in the running page, not inferred from the brief.

Two different questions. Status answers "is it built?" and is judged against the prototypes. The Live app today line on each flow answers "does app.stag.magna.ai do it?" — often a harder no, and the reason the last section of this page exists.

  • The live build has no maths renderer, no diagram renderer, no code copy button and no copy-full-answer (R1–R4).
  • No document, spreadsheet or deck export exists anywhere — live or prototype.
  • Live projects carry no files and no instructions; the prototypes' projects carry both.
