02 — Sovereignty, concretely

Follow one prompt from the keyboard to the answer.

Sovereignty is not a badge on a slide. It is a claim about a specific path taken by a specific packet. Here is the whole path on a private-tier turn.

01

The prompt is written

A user types into MagnaVERSE from a browser on the corporate network. The session never leaves your identity boundary.

Origin · your network
02

Context is retrieved

Company Knowledge, connectors and the digital-twin context graph are queried in place. Documents are read where they already live.

Retrieval · in-VPC
03

Inference executes

An open-weight model runs on Magna GPUs — a typical tenancy is 8×H200 in Riyadh DC-1, or your own POD on your own floor.

Compute · in-country
04

The answer returns

The response, its citations and the full audit record are written to storage you control. Egress on the turn: zero bytes.

Result · logged, retained by you

Stays inside the perimeter

  • Prompts, attachments and conversation history
  • Model weights — the models are hosted, not called
  • Embeddings and the retrieval index
  • Connector traffic: SAP S/4HANA, Primavera P6, Jira, Confluence, mail via MCP
  • The digital-twin context graph and every document that feeds it
  • Audit trails, policy decisions and retention

Leaves on a private-tier turn

  • Nothing.
  • No prompt text to a third-party API
  • No telemetry containing customer content
  • No training signal — your data does not improve anyone's model
  • No vendor-side copy to be requested by a foreign process
  • Public-tier routing exists, but it is off unless a user explicitly confirms it, and the confirmation is logged

The test to apply: ask any AI vendor to name the physical machine that executes your prompt, and who can legally compel access to it. MagnaVERSE answers with a rack number.

05 — Security & compliance

Built by the security half of the joint venture.

“Name the machine that runs the prompt. Everything else is a detail.”

Magna AI is a joint venture of Trend Micro and Wistron, powered by NVIDIA. One partner has spent three decades in enterprise threat defence; the other builds the hardware. That is an unusual pedigree for an AI workspace, and it shows up in the controls rather than the marketing.

ISO 27001 aligned Red-team audited Five global regions 24/7 managed operations In-country residency

Data residency, guaranteed

Inference and storage are pinned to a named in-country facility. The residency claim is a location, not a policy setting.

Audit trail on every turn

Who asked, which model answered, which sources were read, which tier was used and which policy applied — exportable for the regulator or the internal auditor.

Sovereignty tiers, enforced

Private is the default and blocks egress outright. Any public-tier route demands an explicit user confirmation and is recorded — no silent fallback.

Red-team audited platform

Prompt-injection, tool-abuse and data-exfiltration paths are tested adversarially, with connector scopes constrained to least privilege by default.

Five global regions

For groups operating beyond the Kingdom, each region holds its own residency boundary. Multinational structure without a single global data pool.

24/7 managed operations

Magna runs the POD, the models and the upgrades. Your team keeps the data, the policy and the audit record.

06 — Deployment

Three ways to own the boundary.

The workspace is identical in all three. What changes is where the compute physically sits and who racks it — which is usually the only question the security review actually has.
