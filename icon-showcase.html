MagnaVERSE Icon System

Five futuristic icon directions for the AI OS. Each is designed for the Console desktop environment — scalable, consistent, and premium at every size.

Review & approve one direction

Direction 1 — Neural Circuit

Thin strokes with circuit-node endpoints. Each icon feels like a fragment of a neural network.

Recommended

Design Principles

  • 1.5px uniform stroke weight
  • Small circles at line endpoints (circuit nodes)
  • Open paths — never fully closed shapes
  • Consistent 2px padding from viewBox edge
  • Rounded linecaps and linejoins

Why This Works

  • Feels technical and precise — matches the AI OS identity
  • Circuit nodes suggest connectivity and intelligence
  • Thin strokes stay clean at 13px (dock) and 48px (desktop)
  • Glow effect on hover is natural — nodes "light up"
  • Differentiates from macOS/Windows iconography

Direction 2 — Glass Molecules

Rounded geometric shapes with translucent fills. Each icon is a "molecule" of the glass design system.

Alternative

Design Principles

  • 2px stroke with rounded caps
  • Translucent fill on primary shape (10% opacity)
  • Compound shapes — circle + path combinations
  • Corner radius matches the OS window radius
  • Inner detail uses thinner 1.2px strokes

Why This Works

  • Matches the glass/blur system already in Console
  • Translucent fills create depth without heaviness
  • Familiar enough for enterprise users
  • Each icon reads clearly as a filled shape at small sizes
  • Natural hover state — fill opacity increases

Direction 3 — Holographic Wireframe

3D wireframe aesthetic with holographic gradient strokes. Each icon looks like a hologram projection.

Alternative

Design Principles

  • 1.2px strokes with linear gradient stroke
  • Isometric or 3/4 perspective hints
  • Gradient: cyan → violet → emerald (brand tricolor)
  • Dashed secondary lines for "projection" feel
  • No fills — pure wireframe

Why This Works

  • Most futuristic of all directions
  • Gradient strokes tie directly to Magna brand colors
  • Wireframe feel = technical precision
  • Strong differentiation from all competitors
  • Risk: may be harder to read at 13px

Direction 4 — Monoline Geometric

Consistent 1.8px monoline with bold geometric abstraction. Clean, minimal, enterprise-grade.

Alternative

Design Principles

  • 1.8px uniform stroke — no variation
  • Strict geometric primitives (circles, squares, lines)
  • No fills at all — pure stroke
  • Maximum simplification — 3-4 strokes per icon
  • Grid-aligned endpoints (pixel-perfect)

Why This Works

  • Most readable at tiny sizes (13px dock)
  • Feels professional and enterprise-ready
  • Easiest to maintain and extend
  • Low visual noise — calm and minimal
  • Risk: may feel too simple for "futuristic" brief

Direction 5 — Quantum Dots

Dot-based icons where small circles form the shapes. Represents data, vectors, and the RAG system.

Alternative

Design Principles

  • Icons built from 2-3px dots (circles)
  • Thin 1px lines connecting dots
  • Dots are the primary visual — lines are secondary
  • Each dot can glow independently on hover
  • Represents the vector/embedding concept literally

Why This Works

  • Directly references the RAG/vector concept
  • Unique — no other OS uses dot-based icons
  • Hover animation is natural — dots pulse sequentially
  • Ties the icon system to the Brain identity
  • Risk: may be too abstract for some app names
